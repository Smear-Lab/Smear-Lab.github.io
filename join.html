We are looking for people who want to watch animals behave carefully and then take the measurement problem seriously. Backgrounds vary: the lab has drawn from neuroscience, psychology, biology, engineering, and music. What is shared is a tolerance for looking at raw data for a long time before summarizing it.

Graduate students

Matt is accepting graduate students for Fall 2026. Students typically enter through the University of Oregon's Psychology, Biology, or Neuroscience programs. If you are applying, write to Matt before the deadline and say what you want to work on.

University of Oregon undergraduates have run substantial projects here and appear as authors on lab papers. Write to Matt with a sentence about what interests you and when you are free.

Everyone

Matt cofounded NICE in Neuro, and this lab tries to work the way NICE argues labs should. If there is something that would make working here workable for you, say so early, and it will be treated as a design problem rather than an accommodation request.

Write to smear@uoregon.edu. Put something specific in the subject line so it does not read as bulk mail.
