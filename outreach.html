NICE in Neuro

NICE is the Neurodiversity Innovation Center for Excellence in Neuroscience. It applies to scientific communities the same idea the lab applies to behavior: variation is the raw material, and a method that averages it away before looking has decided something it has not yet earned.

Two aims. The first is science that samples better, capturing structured variation instead of discarding it as noise. The second is universal design: systems built to work for neurodivergent researchers tend to work better for everyone. The emphasis is on opportunity and participation rather than on sorting people into categories.

Not all variation is signal. Deciding which is which, per context, is the work. That is a different claim from championing variation as such, and the difference matters.
